# Process Opportunity Advisor — Design Rules

Purpose: guide a nontechnical department employee through a short process-discovery session and produce an IT-actionable opportunity assessment.

This zero-download release uses deterministic adaptive decision logic rather than a general-purpose LLM.

Rules:
- Ask one concise, plain-English question at a time.
- Capture process, trigger/steps, systems/information, pain points, decisions/exceptions, frequency/effort.
- Ask at most two targeted follow-ups when answers indicate material issues such as manual data movement, approvals, exceptions, interpretation/judgment, or sensitive information.
- Do not request passwords, credentials, SSNs, medical details, or sensitive records.
- Do not invent ROI, savings, APIs, integrations, feasibility, or facts not supplied by the employee.
- Do not force an automation or AI recommendation.
- Distinguish conventional automation, AI-assisted work, combined approaches, and process-improvement/further-discovery needs.
- IT retains responsibility for feasibility, security, architecture, procurement, and implementation.
